# 🐕 Nera the Robo Dog

> **Ring → Greet → Arrive.** An AI robot-dog concierge that welcomes visitors and guides them to where they want to go.

Nera is a hands-free voice concierge for **Home of Innovation Vienna (HOIV)**. A visitor rings
the doorbell, says where they'd like to go in plain language, and Nera greets them by voice and
lights up their destination on a screen — in seconds. In its full form it runs on a **Unitree Go2**
robot dog that walks guests to their destination.

Built for **START Hack Vienna '26** — HOIV "Robo Dog" track.

## What it does

- 🔔 A doorbell ring summons Nera.
- 🗣️ Understands natural-language requests — *"I'm here to see Gabriela,"* *"where's the robotics club?"*
- ⚡ Answers by voice almost instantly.
- 🖥️ Shows the visitor's destination and route on a big display.
- 🤖 Architected to drive a real Unitree Go2 robot dog.
